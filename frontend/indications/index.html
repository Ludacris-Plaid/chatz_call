THE WEB
SHOULD BE
EXTRAORDINARY

We are a small, focused studio based in Edmonton, Alberta. We do not build templates. We do not ship generic themes. Every site we produce is conceived, designed, and developed from scratch — purpose-built for the business it represents and the people it serves.

Our practice is grounded in craft over speed. We believe a website earns its keep through clarity, character, and conviction. That means saying no to trends, no to bloated frameworks, and no to anything that does not serve the visitor's intent.

We work with businesses that care about how they are perceived — owners who understand that the difference between a good site and a great one is the difference between a look and a lasting impression.

[ HOW WE WORK ]

01

DISCOVERY

We begin by understanding your business, your market, and your customers. Through structured interviews and competitive analysis, we identify what sets you apart and what your audience needs most from your site. This research becomes the foundation of every decision that follows.

02

DESIGN

Working from the research phase, we produce a tailored visual direction — typography, palette, layout patterns, and interaction design. Each decision is intentional. We present options, discuss trade-offs, and refine until the design feels inevitable, not arbitrary.

03

BUILD

We hand-code your site using modern, semantic HTML5, CSS custom properties, and performant JavaScript. No page builders. No drag-and-drop. The result is a fast, accessible, responsive site that scores well on every technical benchmark that matters.

04

LAUNCH & SUPPORT

Before launch, we run a complete quality assurance pass — cross-browser testing, performance profiling, accessibility audit, and content review. After launch, you receive ongoing maintenance and priority support. Your site stays current, secure, and fast.
